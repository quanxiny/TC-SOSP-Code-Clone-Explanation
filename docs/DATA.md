# Explanation data notes

GCJ natural pairs come from the fragment-disjoint validation split. Project
CodeNet data follows the separately frozen timestamp and anonymized-user group
protocol. The included compact transformation sets provide the exact 30 GCJ
and 30 CodeNet programs used for positive transformation truth and the 15
CodeNet pairs used for negative truth.

Identifier renaming preserves program semantics and yields auditable node
correspondence. A zero-valued guarded empty-output block introduces three known
irrelevant CFG nodes. All variants compile, and their file and graph hashes are
recorded in the manifests.
